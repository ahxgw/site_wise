Daily Site Profile

dcinside.com

DCinside is Korea's fifth-ranked site: 93K anonymous galleries whose robots.txt invites every crawler while its terms of service forbid crawling entirely.

Community Forum Korean Anonymous UGC

Evidence: verified inferred (must state basis) open — conventions in the methodology.

Context

DCinside opened in October 1999 as a digital-camera review board — the name is "Digital Camera Inside" — and became the archetype of Korean anonymous internet culture. Its unit is the gallery: a topic board in one of four tiers (major, minor, mini, person), 93K in total, almost all user-created. Posting needs no account; anonymous authors are labelled with the first two octets of their IP instead of a name. Revenue is advertising and DCCon stickers on a 32-person staff, and in 2026 H Private Equity closed a ~₩200B buyout of parent Community Connect. For an index builder the site is contradictory in a useful way: it publishes clean DiscussionForumPosting JSON-LD on every post, opens robots.txt to all non-AI agents, and ships five sitemaps — then bans crawling outright in its terms and throttles automated clients to zero-byte responses. A large Korean-language opinion corpus with a wide-open front door and a contract nailed to it.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where DCinside differs from the default platform assumptions.

Identifier & dedup

The key is (gallery id, no) — no is a per-gallery counter, so post numbers collide across galleries. Each post has four URL forms by tier (/board/, /mgallery/board/, /mini/board/, /person/board/) plus the mobile alias m.dcinside.com/board/{id}/{no}. Canonicalize on <link rel="canonical">, not the JSON-LD, which appends a stray &page=.

Gallery IDs are period-scoped

High-volume galleries roll over: the domestic-baseball board is titled "202206~202512 국내야구 갤러리" with baseball_new12–14 already created and the retired baseball_new8 blocked in robots.txt. One topic spans a chain of gallery IDs, so the gallery is not the stable entity — the topic is, and it needs its own id-chain table.

Retrieval unit

The post is the addressable unit but not where the content is. Sampled bodies ran 1–1,284 characters while one post carried 6,349 comments, and site-wide comments run 2.44M/day. Index the post as a thread head and the comment thread as the payload, or you index almost nothing.

Authoritative content field

JSON-LD articleBody is the clean extraction target at 18/18 coverage. But it is post-only: comments never appear in the crawled document, so an HTML-only crawler silently records every thread as empty. That is the biggest trap on this site, and the zero-byte throttle is the second — treat a zero-length body on a 200 as a hard fault, not an empty page.

Identity & anonymity

Three author classes: 고정닉 (fixed nickname, has a gallog URL), 비고정닉 (non-unique nickname), and 유동닉 (no account, shown as two IP octets). 93 of 106 sampled comments had no user_id. Author-level aggregation works only for the gallog-bearing minority; the partial-IP fallback is personal data that should not be retained.

Freshness & recrawl

Sitemap lastmod is the fetch date on all 93K entries and carries no information, so recrawl must be driven by board-list polling. Budget for tombstones: 23% of sampled post IDs are gone while 2022 posts still render intact — deletion concentrates early rather than spreading over time.

Internal vs external search

search.dcinside.com serves cross-gallery post search and has no robots.txt at all (404), so it is unconstrained by the site's own crawl policy — the one surface where post-level discovery is cheap. Mobile robots.txt meanwhile disallows /search/, so the permission is inconsistent across hosts.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: thread = post (JSON-LD articleBody) + its full comment list, stored as one document with comments as sub-records.
  • Primary keys & joins: (tier, gallery_id, no) for posts; (gallery_id, no, comment_no) for comments; gallog handle for the identified-author minority; a maintained topic→gallery-id-chain table to survive period rollovers.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) Written consent under Art. 16 — the only licensed path, and the only one that also unlocks AI-training use; no request channel is published, which is an Open Question. 2) search.dcinside.com for post discovery, the sole surface with no robots constraint. 3) Board-list polling, 93,230 boards deep, throttle-limited. 4) The comment endpoint at two requests per page. Sitemaps are a gallery directory and give no post coverage at all.
  • Recrawl cadence: comments on hot threads hourly for their first day; post bodies once, then deletion checks only; board lists on an activity-weighted schedule; the gallery directory monthly. Ignore lastmod.
  • Derivatives to generate: Korean slang and neologism normalization (the corpus is the source of much of it, so stock tokenizers underperform); OCR over image posts, since many are an image with a one-character body; a tombstone table keyed on the 404s; DCCon sticker recognition so sticker-only comments are not indexed as text.
  • Demo to build: a throttle-aware thread fetcher that pulls a post plus its full comment thread through the e_s_n_o handshake, emits one JSONL document per thread, and flags zero-byte 200s as blocks rather than empty pages — showing how many documents a naive crawler silently loses.

Risks to the Index

  • robots.txt and the terms disagree, and the terms win. robots.txt says User-agent: * / Allow: /; Article 16 prohibits crawling in any form for any purpose without prior written consent and threatens civil and criminal liability. An index built on the permissive reading rests on the weaker document.
  • The company reserves AI-training rights it denies everyone else. Article 14 §3 lets DCinside train on user content without author consent and pass it to media partners, while Article 16 §2 withholds the same use from third parties even for non-profit research. Expect commercial negotiation, not an opt-in program.
  • robots.txt doubles as a takedown ledger. The 14 galleries, ~30 named posts and gallog handles disallowed there are suppression requests, so an index that ignores robots.txt inherits exactly the exposure the site was shedding — and the list grows without notice.
  • inferred Ownership change points toward tighter, monetized access. Basis: H PE closed a ~₩200B buyout at roughly 7× FY2025 revenue (calculated by Site Wise from Verified Metrics) with a stated "Korean Reddit" plan, and the crawling clause was carried through two consecutive 2026 terms revisions observed in the Probe Log.
  • inferred Comment records carry personal data. Basis: the comment API returns partial author IPs for anonymous posters, 87 of 106 sampled records, which puts retention under Korean privacy law rather than mere terms risk.
