Context

TradingView is a browser charting platform with a social network attached. It was founded in 2011 and went through Techstars in 2013. It raised $37M in 2018 and $298M at a $3B valuation in October 2021 (Wikipedia, PR Newswire). For an index builder it is three corpora on one host. The first is an instrument catalogue: about 1M symbol pages, each with schema.org FinancialProduct JSON-LD that carries ISIN, CUSIP and FIGI. That makes it a free cross-exchange join table. The second is a user-generated layer of ideas (annotated charts with prose) and Pine Script indicators, which share one numeric ID sequence that has passed 23.8M. The third is a licensed newswire mirror. Most of it is server-rendered and crawlable. The binding constraint is contractual: the terms license all content and market data for "display-only use" and forbid "any processing of TradingView's content".

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where tradingview.com differs from the default platform assumptions.

AI blocks guard the community, not the catalogue

Most platforms block AI crawlers wholesale. TradingView blocks 16 named agents only from ideas, scripts, profiles, Minds and chat, and leaves symbol, market, news and support pages open. The line it draws is user-authored versus TradingView-authored, which tells an index which half of the corpus the owner will defend.

The symbol key needs a crosswalk

The URL key is {EXCHANGE}-{TICKER}; APIs use EXCHANGE:TICKER, and tickers contain ! (continuous futures, 8.5K of them), .P (perpetuals) and /. One issuer fans out to hundreds of symbols: AAPL search reports 188 more. Normalize to ISIN or FIGI when present (about two thirds of pages) and keep is_primary_listing from symbol search to collapse cross-listings. For indices, COT and on-chain series the ticker is the only key.

The retrieval unit is the idea, not the page

An idea is prose plus a chart state plus a symbol plus a direction and timeframe. The content JSON holds the exact drawings and indicators, so "head-and-shoulders on BTC weekly" can be matched structurally instead of via OCR of the PNG. Symbol /ideas/ tabs canonicalize to the symbol page, so crawl them for discovery and index only the detail pages.

The sitemap is a showcase, not an inventory

UGC sitemaps list about 6K evergreen hits (the 2019 BTC fractal, the GME squeeze) against a 23.8M ID space. Listing pages stop at 12K. Coverage has to come from graph expansion: symbol tabs, author profiles, tag pages (163 in the sitemap) and the recent stream polled faster than 12K items accumulate. An ID sweep also works, since IDs are dense and numeric, but the UUID in the URL is not derivable from the ID (open).

Quality signals are exposed

Every idea record carries is_picked (Editors' Pick), is_reviewed, is_hot, an author reputation, is_platinum_broker_idea and a views, likes and comments triple. The spam vectors are broker-account ideas and self-promoting signatures (Telegram links in user_signature_socials). Down-rank on signature links and posting bursts (one account posted 4 ideas in 12 minutes).

Query profile

Queries are ticker-first ("NVDA", "XAUUSD", "BTC dominance"), then pattern and strategy terms ("SMC order block", "Elliott wave count"), then indicator names ("Range Filter", "Supertrend"). On-site symbol search is strong, but ideas search (/ideas/search/) is robots-disallowed, which leaves room for external retrieval.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: the instrument (identifiers, name, type, exchange, sector path, description link), and the idea or script (id + uuid: title, description, symbol, interval, direction, author, timestamps, flags, counts, chart-state features, thumbnail). News is headline-level only.
  • Primary keys & joins: ISIN, FIGI and CUSIP ↔ EXCHANGE:TICKER ↔ CIK (from symbol search), which joins to SEC EDGAR. Idea pro_symbol links to the instrument. Idea id is the primary key, with uuid the URL key. Script script_id_part is the source key. News relatedSymbols links to instruments.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) The symbol sitemap weekly (27 shards) plus JSON-LD extraction, for about 1.04M entities; roughly 245GB for the first pass. 2) The recent-ideas and scripts streams polled every 15–30 minutes, with detail pages parsed through ssrIdeaData. 3) Graph expansion over symbol tabs, profiles and tag pages for the back catalogue. 4) pine-facade/get for open-script source. 5) The news-flow JSON for headline and symbol links. No price or fundamental values are stored, only the entity graph.
  • Recrawl cadence: the symbol sitemap weekly, with the diff giving listings and delistings. Ideas: new ones within 30 minutes; counters at 1, 7 and 30 days, then quarterly. Scripts on a version change. News: headline polling every 5 minutes.
  • Derivatives to generate: pattern and indicator features parsed from the chart-state JSON; embeddings of idea prose keyed to symbol and timeframe; an outcome label, joining idea direction and created_at against later price. That label is computed locally for research only, and it touches market data, so it needs a licensed price source other than TradingView. For Pine source: AST and code embeddings for indicator similarity.
  • Demo to build: an identifier crosswalk. Enter an ISIN, FIGI or CIK and get every TradingView symbol URL for that security, grouped by exchange, with the primary listing flagged. It would be built from sitemap plus JSON-LD plus symbol search, using no market-data values.

Risks to the Index

  • The terms bar processing, not just redistribution. Section 3 licenses all "content and market data" for "display-only use" and prohibits "any processing of TradingView's content" and building products on it (Terms of Use). Identifiers are facts, but ideas, descriptions and prices fall inside the clause. Treat the index as a link-and-snippet discovery layer, and get legal review before any embedding or derivative of UGC.
  • The UGC half is closed to AI agents by robots. A crawler identifying as an AI agent must skip ideas and scripts entirely. A search crawler may fetch them, but its output then must not feed model training. Keep crawl identity and downstream use aligned.
  • Keyless backends are internal plumbing. Scanner, symbol-search, news-mediator and pine-facade answer anonymous calls today. The paid MCP server (2026-09-16) is now the sanctioned programmatic path, which makes lockdown of the anonymous ones more likely. Treat them as optional.
  • Licensed news can disappear by provider. Per-story noindex (Dow Jones) and permission tiers show the contracts vary by provider. A license change can remove a provider's back catalogue at once; store provider-level tombstones.

Inferences

  • About 95% of new IDs never reach the public English stream: they are private or unpublished charts, other locales, or moderated-out posts. Basis: 455 IDs consumed against 22 listed ideas in the same 2.5h window, is_public/is_visible flags in every record, and separate locale sitemaps with their own idea lists.
  • Recent listings are ordered by publication or moderation time, not ID. Basis: ID 23,837,777 (created 2026-09-29 07:57) is interleaved with IDs from 18h later, and records carry is_reviewed.
  • FINRA bond pages leave the site at maturity as hard 404s rather than soft redirects. Basis: FINRA-AAPL5000369 returned a 404 while 60 of 60 current sitemap URLs returned 200.

Open Questions

  • How many ideas and scripts are public in total, and in which languages. Could be closed by sampling 1,000 random IDs in the 1–23.8M range through an ID-resolving endpoint, if one exists, or by counting via each locale's listing and profile pages.
  • Whether a numeric ID can be resolved to its uuid URL without a listing. Could be closed by testing /chart/?id=-style and internal JSON endpoints observed in the idea page's JS bundles.
  • The split of the script corpus between open, protected and invite-only. Could be closed by calling pine-facade/get on 500 script IDs collected from listings and tallying scriptAccess.
  • The rate limits on symbol pages and the keyless backends. Could be closed by a controlled ramp from 1 to 5 requests per second per host over 10 minutes, watching for 429, 403 or CloudFront blocks.
  • How much of the symbol corpus is duplicated across the 20 locale hosts beyond the hreflang set. Could be closed by a census of each locale's symbol sitemap and a diff of symbol IDs against www.
  • An independent, dated user or MAU figure. Could be closed by a primary disclosure (funding press release or regulatory filing) or a named third-party panel.
