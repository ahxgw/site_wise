Context

indiatimes.com is the Times Group's umbrella domain. Its apex 301s to www.indiatimes.com, a viral and trending desk, while the traffic weight sits on timesofindia.indiatimes.com, the website of The Times of India. The paper was founded on Nov 3 1838 as The Bombay Times and Journal of Commerce and is published by Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd (BCCL); its digital properties are run by the subsidiary Times Internet. Revenue is advertising plus the TOI+ subscription tier. For an index builder, the two hosts are one corpus: both publish from the same Times CMS numeric msid sequence (Probe Log), so this report treats them together. The delta from other news sites is how open it is. Every article page carries the full body in JSON-LD, the sitemap tree enumerates the archive month by month back to 2000, and robots.txt blocks no AI crawler except Meta's. The catch is at the edge, not in policy: Akamai refuses GPTBot outright while serving ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and CCBot the same bytes as a browser.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where indiatimes.com departs from the default platform assumptions.

Identifier & dedup

Key everything on (host, msid). The slug is decorative: TOI resolves /articleshow/{msid}.cms without it, so slug rewrites after headline edits do not create new documents. msids are global across the Times CMS (articles, images, videos, both hosts) but are not routable across hosts, so the host stays part of the key. AMP twins and the 11 overlapping category indexes are duplicates of the news tree; collapse them to the canonical.

Retrieval unit

The article, with live blogs split into timestamped updates. Desk bylines (TNN, Times News Network) are not authors; treat them as a wire flag and keep Person authors (www.indiatimes.com, and named TOI reporters) as entities.

Authoritative content field

NewsArticle.articleBody beats the rendered DOM: it is present on every sampled page and free of ad slots and related-story widgets. Two caveats: on TOI+ it is a teaser, so gate on isAccessibleForFree before calling a document complete; and VideoObject.datePublished is not ISO 8601, so parse it as local IST text or fall back to the sitemap lastmod.

Freshness & recrawl

The sitemap tree is a complete changelog, unusual for a publisher this size: daily files for discovery, monthly parts for backfill, both with lastmod. Throttled sitemap fetches can come back as empty 200s (Probe Log), so a zero-URL part must be retried before being trusted.

Quality & spam

Two different editorial grades share one domain. www.indiatimes.com is a viral/trending desk whose output fell by more than half since 2024 (Verified Metrics); TOI is the newsroom. Index them as separate sources with separate trust priors, not as one site.

Crawler policy vs edge policy

robots.txt is not the policy here. It allows GPTBot, but Akamai denies the GPTBot UA on everything, including robots.txt itself; Bytespider gets a soft-200 block page that looks like success unless the size is checked (Probe Log). Any crawler must log status and body size per UA.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: article (live-blog updates as children); lead image and video as linked siblings.
  • Primary keys & joins: (host, msid); image msid from the thumb/msid-{id} URL; video joined by page msid → Slike id in contentUrl; news:keywords as entity hints.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) Daily news sitemaps every 15 minutes for discovery, then page fetch for JSON-LD; covers new TOI and www.indiatimes.com output with no auth. 2) Monthly news parts for the 6.7M-URL backfill, at a pace that avoids empty-200 throttling. 3) Section RSS (62 feeds) as a byline-rich low-latency backup. 4) Slideshow parts for galleries. 5) TOI+ full text: subscription or licence only.
  • Recrawl cadence: new articles at 1 h, 24 h and 7 d to catch headline and slug edits; live blogs every 15 minutes while live; archive only when a monthly part's lastmod changes; utility price pages daily.
  • Derivatives to generate: text embeddings from articleBody; ASR on video only after a rights decision, since no captions exist; image embeddings from 1280×720 resizer output (watermarked).
  • Demo to build: a msid timeline: sample the monthly sitemap parts, plot msid against lastmod from 2000 to 2026, and estimate how much of the CMS id space is articles versus images, videos and unpublished items.

Risks to the Index

  • inferred Edge rules can change faster than robots.txt. Basis: the GPTBot 403 and Bytespider block page are enforced at Akamai with no matching robots.txt rule (Probe Log). Another UA could be added the same way without any published signal.
  • inferred Paywall scope can expand. Basis: TOI+ already strips articleBody to a teaser with a .paywall hasPart marker (Probe Log). Moving a section under /toi-plus/ would silently turn full-text documents into teasers; re-check isAccessibleForFree on recrawl.
  • www.indiatimes.com history is gone from discovery. Its sitemap starts in Jan 2024 and robots.txt disallows every year path from 2011 to 2024 (Probe Log); pre-2024 content needs Common Crawl or Wayback, not the site.

Inferences

  • Both hosts share one CMS id space. Basis: www.indiatimes.com article 135,285,875 and TOI article 135,286,740 were published within an hour on Oct 29 2026, and TOI image msids interleave the same sequence (Probe Log).
  • The TOI homepage is a pre-rendered file pushed to object storage, not rendered per request. Basis: x-rgw-object-type, x-amz-request-id and x-amz-meta-* response headers, with the original origin response headers stored as object metadata (2026-10-29).
  • The GPTBot block is deliberate, not a generic bot rule. Basis: 12 other crawler UAs, including OAI-SearchBot and ChatGPT-User from the same operator, received the identical 66,254-byte page (2026-10-29).
