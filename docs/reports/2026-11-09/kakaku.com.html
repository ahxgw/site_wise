Context

kakaku.com (価格.com) is Japan's main price-comparison and buying-guide site, launched in 1997. It is run by Kakaku.com, Inc. (TSE 2371), which also operates Tabelog. It earns from shop click and sales commissions (each shop link goes through a c.kakaku.com/forwarder redirect), listing fees, ads, and lead generation for finance, insurance and telecom. The 価格.com segment is now the group's smaller, shrinking business: revenue fell in H1 FY2027/3 while Tabelog grew. For an index builder it is the opposite of Tabelog. There is no sitemap, but product pages, user reviews, a discussion board (クチコミ掲示板) and daily price histories all answer an honest research UA. Every object sits on a dense sequential ID, and robots.txt now admits GPTBot and CCBot, both of which it blocked in 2024. The constraint is contractual: the terms limit use to private, non-commercial purposes and bar storing information without consent.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where kakaku.com departs from the default platform assumptions.

Identifier & dedup

The product key is K + 10 zero-padded digits, shared across the PC page, s.kakaku.com, review, BBS, price history and the image path. Reviews (ReviewCD) and posts (SortID) carry global counters, but their URLs always nest under the product key. Colour variants are separate K-IDs (the Dyson sample is "[ホワイト/ニッケル]"), so group variants by maker + model string before merging reviews.

No sitemap, dense IDs

No robots version since 2015 names a sitemap, and the usual paths 404. Instead, about 80% of the K-ID space is live and deletions return 410, so a paced walk over 1.8M IDs is a complete census. At 1 req/s that takes about 21 days, calculated by Site Wise. Walk newest-first, because recent IDs carry today's prices.

Encoding trap

Pages are Shift_JIS but item pages omit the charset header. Decode as CP932, not strict SHIFT_JIS: the latter maps byte 0x5C to ¥, which corrupts the JSON-LD escapes ("2025\/06\/21" became "2025¥/06¥/21" in the probe).

Open to AI crawlers

Unlike its sibling Tabelog, which 403s non-search crawlers, kakaku.com dropped its GPTBot and CCBot blocks during 2025–26 while adding Amazon's shopping bots (Probe Log). Its block list is aimed at commerce rivals rather than LLM crawlers.

Authoritative fields

Use JSON-LD lowPrice as the headline price and the inline chartData as the time series. The JAN code is not in JSON-LD; it leaks only inside retailer forwarder URLs, so extract it there to join with other retailers. Review text in JSON-LD is complete, so render the page only for sub-scores.

Quality & spam

Each forwarder link carries a Hash= signature and click-tracking IDs, so never follow it from a crawler: it bills the shop. Reviews show a "pro review" icon on some entries (the newest review sample), so separate sponsored or expert reviews from consumer ones before training rankers.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: the product (K-ID) with brand, category path, spec table, current low price and 2-year price series, plus reviews and BBS threads as children.
  • Primary keys & joins: K-ID internally; JAN from forwarder URLs to join with Amazon, Rakuten and Yahoo Shopping; maker ID (pdf_ma) and category code (CategoryCD/ItemCD) as a taxonomy.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) A data agreement with Kakaku, required by ToS Art. 4/13 for any commercial index, and its terms may change after the tender. 2) A newest-first ID walk of /item/ plus /pricehistory/ for entities and prices. 3) review. and bbs. detail pages, discovered from item pages, for text. 4) Common Crawl WARCs for one-off research samples.
  • Recrawl cadence: prices for top categories daily (the history page backfills gaps); new product IDs daily from the counter tip; reviews and BBS daily from the newest-ID list pages; the full ID space quarterly for 410 tombstones.
  • Derivatives to generate: a JAN ↔ K-ID crosswalk; price-drop and launch-price curves per category; facet-level review summaries from the 【…】 headings.
  • Demo to build: a "buy now or wait" predictor. Pull chartData for ~1K products in one category and fit days-since-registration against price decay, using only the open price-history pages.

Risks to the Index

  • inferred Access may tighten to Tabelog's level after the take-private. Basis: the same owner already serves a selective 403 on Tabelog's Japanese pages (Tabelog report), the buyer's thesis is reducing SEO dependence, and this site's robots.txt has changed AI-crawler stanzas three times in two years (Probe Log).
  • inferred Open access is not permission. Basis: robots.txt admits generic crawlers while ToS Art. 4 and 13 forbid commercial use and storage without consent (Verified Metrics); for a commercial index the contract, not the crawl policy, decides.
