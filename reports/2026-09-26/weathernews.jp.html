Context

weathernews.jp is the consumer web front of Weathernews Inc., a Chiba-based private forecaster founded in 1986 (Wikipedia). Most revenue comes from shipping, aviation and land-business clients. The consumer app and site form the "Internet Domain", which earns from subscriptions, ads and carrier deals. The site offers three kinds of content. Point forecasts are thin HTML shells filled by a JSON API. About 23 staff-written weather news articles appear each day. Its most distinctive asset is Weather Report (ウェザーリポート): geotagged sky photos with a short comment, sent in by users since November 2005. The company says it feeds these into its forecasts. For an index builder, the photos are the unusual part. Every report ever submitted, back to ID 1M in January 2009, still resolves at a predictable URL with its full-size image. The ID counts reports cumulatively, which the company's own "100M reports" press release confirms. None of it appears in any sitemap, and the photo host blocks named AI crawlers entirely.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where weathernews.jp differs from the default platform assumptions.

The robots split is by host

On weathernews.jp the four AI agents are blocked only from /a/repo/, a legacy report path, and are otherwise less restricted than *. On wxrepo., where the reports actually live, they are blocked from everything. Treat news and forecast pages as open to AI crawlers and the photo corpus as closed to them. A crawler that checks only the apex robots.txt will get this wrong.

Two counters instead of a sitemap

The sitemaps cover only evergreen spot pages. News and reports are reachable only by counter. A news ID is a day plus a serial, with a generation digit that changes across eras, so backfilling means probing about 10 variants a day unless the digit for that era is known. A report ID is a single global sequence: iterating it enumerates the whole corpus, and ID gaps are private or removed reports. The date in the image path must match the report date, so image URLs cannot be built from the ID alone.

Authoritative fields

For news, take the headline from <title> or og:title and drop the " - ウェザーニュース" suffix. The JSON-LD headline is the description. Take the date from datePublished, not from the ID, because evergreen pages are republished. For reports, the page <title> is the user comment and place is the city, with no coordinates on the page. The ID date and the image path give the day. The listing gives only "MM/DD hh:mm", with no year.

Forecast pages carry no forecast

The /onebox/tenki/… HTML is a static S3 object last modified 9 days ago. An HTML-only crawl indexes navigation and boilerplate. The values come from api_data_ss1 by lat/lon, keyed to JCODE, the national municipality code, which joins cleanly to Japanese government geodata.

Query profile

Users search by place name plus 天気 (weather) and by event (typhoon number, earthquake, 花粉 pollen, 桜 sakura). News titles are templated ("今日X月Y日(曜)の天気予報" for today's forecast, "週間天気予報" for the weekly forecast). Collapse them by template when ranking, or a daily-forecast query returns ten near-duplicates.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: two primaries. One is the news article, keyed by tpid and carrying title, body, datePublished, category and images. The other is the Weather Report, keyed by numeric ID and carrying comment, city, prefecture, date and an image. Forecasts are a live side lookup, not indexed documents.
  • Primary keys & joins: the report's prefecture/city string and a forecast point's JCODE join photos to forecast areas. Report date plus prefecture joins photos to news about the same event, for example a typhoon article and that day's reports from the affected prefecture. The reporter name is an opaque handle.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) News: poll list_m.json daily, which covers 100% of new articles with a 4-day buffer, then fetch each article once. 2) Reports: only with a licence from Weathernews, because of the unresolved contributor-copyright question and the host-wide AI block. Technically it is ID iteration at about 1 request per second. 3) Spot guides: section sitemaps, refreshed weekly in season.
  • Recrawl cadence: news daily from the feed, plus a weekly re-read of the evergreen IDs whose datePublished moves. Reports need one fetch; recheck only for removals. Spot pages daily in their season, otherwise yearly.
  • Derivatives to generate: image embeddings and sky or phenomenon labels (rain, snow, rainbow, flood) on report photos, aligned to date and prefecture, which gives a ground-truth weather image corpus. Collapse templated news titles for deduplication.
  • Demo to build: a "typhoon in photos" timeline. For one storm, take the news tpids, bracket the report ID range by date using the 119-byte redirects plus a binary search on the image date, and plot report counts per prefecture per hour. It turns the counter into a disaster-response signal using only public metadata, without storing photos.

Risks to the Index

  • Photo rights may sit with the contributors. The Terms say outside contributions belong to their authors and need the author's permission as well as Weathernews's. If Weather Reports count as such contributions, a licence from Weathernews alone would not cover showing the images. Until that is settled, keep derived labels and the link and drop the pixels.
  • Crawl pace is enforced by the CDN, not by robots.txt. There is no Crawl-delay. The per-distribution 403 block arrives without warning, and it probably blocks by IP. Budget ≤1 request per second per host.
  • inferred ID formats change with each platform migration. Basis: the news generation digit changed three times (5 → 6 → 1). The last change landed on 2026-04-14 together with the Nuxt news app, whose sitemap is still misconfigured. An ID-based backfill needs revalidation after each redesign.
  • Weathernews is expanding outside Japan. The app went global in August 2026 (Wikipedia), and the forecast API already answers for Paris. Non-Japanese report and page volume may grow on new hosts or paths.

Open Questions

  • Total news archive size, and why 2022-06-08 returned no IDs even though 2022-01, 2022-06-01 and 2022-09 IDs are live. Could be closed by a paced (≤1 request per second) probe of serials 000–030 on one day per quarter in both namespaces, seeded from Wayback CDX prefixes.
  • What share of the ≈121M report IDs still render publicly, since deleted and private reports leave gaps. Could be closed with a random sample of 1,000 IDs through the 119B detail redirect (redirect to /report/ = gone).
  • Who holds the rights in Weather Report photos: the app's posting terms, not the site terms, would settle it. Could be closed by reading the app's user agreement (/ip/kiyaku/).
  • Whether a licensed feed of Weather Reports exists for third parties. The press release mentions B2B and municipal use. Closable only by asking Weathernews.
  • Whether report video exists (the 2025 press release says "photos or videos"). No .mp4 was found on sampled pages; could be closed by sampling reports tagged as video in the app.
  • Rate-limit threshold and block duration. Could be closed with a controlled ramp test from a separate IP: requests per minute until the first 403, then time until the first 200.
