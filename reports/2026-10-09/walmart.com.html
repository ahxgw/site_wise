Context

walmart.com is the US online store of the world's largest retailer. It combines first-party retail, a fast-growing third-party marketplace and store-fulfilled pickup and delivery. Ads (Walmart Connect) and Walmart+ memberships are earned against the same product graph. For an index builder, Walmart is the opposite of Amazon in two ways. First, its robots.txt names no AI crawler at all, yet its edge bot manager blocks every client that reaches origin, Googlebot UA included (Probe Log). Second, its official Affiliate API returns review bodies, a paginated full-catalog feed and UPC/GTIN lookup, which Amazon's PA-API does not. inferred Walmart sets its crawl policy at the edge, not in robots.txt. Basis: robots.txt has no user-agent group for any AI crawler, but ClaudeBot, GPTBot, Googlebot and browser UAs all got the same 307 to /blocked on 2026-10-09.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where Walmart differs from the default platform assumptions.

Identifier & dedup

The key is the numeric usItemId at the end of /ip/. The slug is cosmetic: archived captures show item 13035281 under both Stanley-1000-Amp-… and Stanley-J5C09-500A-…. Strip athena/ath* (on-site recommendation tracking) and Impact parameters (clickid, irgwc, wmlspartner, campaign_id=9383), which fill archive captures. Group variants by the 12-char productId; primaryUsItemId names the family head.

Retrieval unit

As with Amazon, there are three units: the product family (productId) for search, the offer (offerId = item × seller) for price and fulfillment, and the review for text. Walmart adds a fourth: the review aspect (e.g. "Battery Life", 89 snippets), a ready-made facet for opinion retrieval.

Cross-modal joins

gtin13 in JSON-LD and upc in the API join Walmart to Amazon (via PA-API ExternalIds) and to any GTIN-keyed catalogue. Images and video join on usItemId only through page or API payloads; their CDN paths are opaque UUIDs.

Authoritative content field

specifications (name/value pairs) beat the title. Marketplace titles are SEO-stuffed (sitemap slugs run past 150 characters). inferred The review summary and aspects are machine-generated derivatives, not customer text. Basis: the summary is one synthesized paragraph ("Customers praise … However, some users …"), and each aspect carries a snippetCount, not an author.

Freshness & recrawl

Use the per-URL lastmod in itp children; the index-level dates are batch stamps. Families regenerate on different clocks: rp_01 on 2026-09-26, itp_02 on 2026-04-30, brp on 2025-11-18. Price and stock have to come from the API, which reprices by ZIP code.

Quality & spam

Seller provenance matters. Item payloads mix Walmart.com and third-party sellers, and fulfillmentType separates Walmart-fulfilled (FC/STORE) from MARKETPLACE. The API's soldByWmt, fulfilledByWalmart and proSellerItems filters give a trust tier for free.

Internal vs external search

/search is disallowed. Walmart publishes ≤3.2M /c/kp/{keyword} landing pages and ≤12.6M facet pages instead, encoding facet filters in base64 path segments. An external index sees Walmart's keyword choices, not its search engine.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: product family (productId) with child items (usItemId). Offers, review text and review aspects are attached as sub-records.
  • Primary keys & joins: usItemId; family = productId; offer = offerId (item × sellerId); UPC/GTIN-13 for cross-store joins; taxonomy path IDs (1334134_5899871_…) for category.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked:
    1. Affiliate API Catalog Product, walking the taxonomy category by category. This is the only bulk text path, but it skips unnamed categories and has no published quota.
    2. Affiliate lookup and reviews for refresh. Lookup takes 20 IDs per call; reviews come back as paged text with display rights.
    3. Archived sitemaps (Wayback / Common Crawl) as the URL and ID frontier: ≤42M URLs, about 470MB gzipped for the item families alone (calculated by Site Wise from 213 × ~2.2MB).
    4. Archived HTML for JSON-LD and video URLs.
    A live crawl is not a path: every uncached URL hits the bot wall.
  • Recrawl cadence: offers daily through lookup, for priority items only. Item text when the item's lastmod changes (7–10% a month in the sample). Reviews weekly for high-volume items. Taxonomy and stores monthly.
  • Derivatives to generate: ASR on merchant videos (captions are null); image embeddings to dedupe marketplace copies of the same GTIN; a GTIN concordance with Amazon ASINs.
  • Demo to build: a sitemap ↔ API reconciliation. Take one archived itp child (50K IDs), resolve it through the Affiliate lookup in batches of 20, and report how many resolve, are excluded, or are stale. That answers both API category exclusion and sitemap staleness.

Risks to the Index

  • The archive path is eroding: Wayback has no capture for 5 of 34 sitemap indexes, and archived child fetches already hit 307s in 2023 (Probe Log). Frontier discovery depends on third-party archives that the same wall degrades.
  • Category exclusions are undisclosed: the API docs say several categories are excluded and "we can not list them here". Coverage gaps will not announce themselves.
  • Agentic distribution may replace open retrieval: Sparky now runs inside ChatGPT (Verified Metrics). inferred Walmart prefers delivering its catalogue through its own agent over being crawled by others. Basis: robots.txt is silent on AI crawlers while the edge blocks them all (Probe Log), and Walmart chose to keep checkout and cart control in its exit from Instant Checkout (Payments Dive).

Inferences

  • Crawl policy is set at the edge, not in robots.txt. Basis: no AI user-agent groups in robots.txt; identical 307 → /blocked for AI, search-engine and browser UAs on 2026-10-09.
  • Review summary and aspects are machine-generated. Basis: single synthesized summary paragraph and aspects[].snippetCount fields in __NEXT_DATA__.
  • Walmart prefers agent-mediated distribution to open crawl. Basis: edge blocks all AI UAs despite permissive robots.txt; Sparky deployed in ChatGPT after an exit from Instant Checkout reported as being about checkout control.
