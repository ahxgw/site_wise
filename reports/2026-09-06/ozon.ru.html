Context

Ozon started in 1998 as a Russian online bookstore and is now one of the country's two dominant marketplaces alongside Wildberries, monetizing through commissions, fulfillment, advertising, and a fast-growing fintech arm. It listed on Nasdaq in November 2020, was delisted after 2022, and redomiciled from Cyprus to the Kaliningrad special administrative region in 2025; the shares now trade in Moscow as OZON. For an index builder Ozon is the sharpest example in this series of a fully closed retail surface: a 370M-item catalog with no sitemap, no bulk feed, no public product API, and an in-house anti-bot that challenges every web host the company runs. inferred Ozon does not publish an AI-crawler policy because generic crawlers never reach the content the policy would govern. Basis: robots.txt names only Yandex, Googlebot and * — zero GPTBot/ClaudeBot/CCBot-class directives — while every HTML fetch in the Probe Log, under four different user agents, terminated at the same CAPTCHA.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Only where Ozon differs from the default platform assumptions.

Identifier & dedup

The canonical key is the trailing numeric ID in /product/<slug>-<id>/, which equals JSON-LD sku. The bare form /product/<id>/ resolves and canonicalizes back to the slug form, so the slug is decorative and must not enter the dedup key. inferred Ozon's URL space is heavily parameter-polluted. Basis: the 713 distinct Clean-param tokens in the Yandex block — including opaque campaign hashes like 2851ee80 and AKNwC — plus the session and userid parameters visible on archived captures. Only Yandex gets that strip list; everyone else must derive canonical form from <link rel="canonical"> or offers.url, present on every sampled page but costing a fetch per URL variant.

Retrieval unit

The product, not the page. A page is a rendering of one sku plus a lazily-loaded tail (gallery, reviews, seller offers) that a single fetch does not deliver. Budget one document per sku and treat the reviews path as a separate, optional enrichment.

Cross-modal joins

All stable and all numeric: sku ↔ sellerId on the product page; itemId (= sku) ↔ reviewUuid ↔ reviewPuuid on review photos. This is a cleaner join graph than most marketplaces expose — the constraint is reaching it, not parsing it.

Media acquisition inverts

inferred Images are the cheapest modality here and text is the most expensive — the reverse of the usual ordering. Basis: ir.ozone.ru served every requested size unauthenticated with a 1-year cache while all eleven HTML fetches across five hosts were challenged. Derivative thumbnails should use the native c<N>/ and wc<N>/ tokens rather than local resizing.

Authoritative content field

JSON-LD name beats og:title (which is whitespace-padded) and beats og:description, which is frequently marketing filler — one sample read only "Успейте купить по выгодной цене!" ("Hurry and buy at a great price!"). og:type is miscoded as website on product pages, so OG-based type detection will misclassify the whole catalog.

Freshness & recrawl

Three clocks, not one: media is immutable, product text is slow, and offers.price/availability plus aggregateRating move continuously. Since every recrawl costs a challenge solve, poll the cheap JSON-LD rating/price fields and re-render the full page only when they move.

Internal vs external search

On-site search is disallowed to every crawler, and generic agents get the broader Disallow: /search prefix. External discovery therefore runs through category and product pages only — and in a market where Yandex holds the majority of Russian search, the Yandex-only allowances and strip list matter more than the Googlebot block.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: one document per sku, carrying title, description, brand, price/currency/availability, rating aggregate, primary image ID, and sellerId; reviews as child documents keyed by reviewUuid.
  • Primary keys & joins: sku as the root key; sellerId for merchant rollups; itemId + reviewUuid + reviewPuuid to bind review text to review media.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) Seller API — the only unchallenged, documented, terms-clean path, but it returns your own catalog only, so it scales with merchant relationships rather than with crawling. 2) Rendered fetch of product and reviews pages — full fidelity, but requires solving the challenge, which is a terms and cost question before it is a technical one. 3) Media CDN by ID — free and unlimited, but strictly downstream of paths 1–2 for ID discovery. There is no fourth path: no sitemap, no feed, no dump, no partner catalog endpoint was found.
  • Recrawl cadence: media never; product text monthly; price/availability/rating as often as the access budget allows, since these are the only fields that actually move.
  • Derivatives to generate: skip thumbnailing — fetch the CDN's own c<N>/ sizes. Do run OCR on review photos (they carry real product condition evidence that the seller copy omits) and embed title+description for Russian-language retrieval. ASR is not justified until the video corpus is sized.
  • Demo to build: a canonicalizer that takes any Ozon URL and reduces it to sku, scored against the 713-token Clean-param list as ground truth — it turns the one artifact Ozon gives away freely into the dedup rule the rest of the site withholds.

Risks to the Index

  • The challenge is the product boundary. Every path except the Seller API depends on defeating an anti-bot the company actively versions (script_v47_5.js). Such an index is one deploy away from zero coverage, and the legal posture is unfavorable regardless of technique.
  • No sitemap means no coverage metric. Without an enumerable URL set there is no denominator, so "how much of Ozon do we have?" stays unanswerable from outside — a reporting problem, not just an ingestion one.
  • Sanctions and jurisdiction. Post-redomiciliation Ozon is a wholly Russian issuer; payment, contracting and data-transfer routes for a paid or partner feed are constrained in ways that do not apply to other marketplaces in this series.
  • Review media carries personal data. Review objects surface author display names and user photos, and archived URLs leaked userid parameters — retention needs a privacy review before indexing, not after.
  • Yandex dependence cuts both ways. The allowances that make Ozon discoverable are written for one crawler; if that relationship changes, the public surface narrows with no notice to third parties.
