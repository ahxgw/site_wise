mail.ru

mail.ru's front door is closed to every crawler but Google and Yandex; its content subdomains publish full article text under attribution-only terms.

Evidence: verified inferred (must state basis) open — conventions in the methodology.

Context

mail.ru is Russia's original free-email service, launched 1998, grown into the consumer portal of VK, the country's dominant internet holding. Products dropped the domain suffix in branding — "Почта Mail", "Новости Mail" — but the hostnames persist. Revenue is advertising across VK's media surfaces plus paid mail and cloud subscriptions. For a full-modal index the site matters because it is two sites under one name. The root behaves like a login wall: robots.txt disallows everything, and the origin serves the portal only to user-agent strings containing "Googlebot" or "YandexBot". The content subdomains behave like an open publisher — 815K news URLs, an estimated 200M questions on otvet.mail.ru, half a million film entities on kino.mail.ru — served to anonymous clients with complete schema.org payloads, under terms that grant unlimited republication of first-party news in exchange for a link.

Retrieval Notes (site-specific deltas)

Policy shape

The methodology's default two-track split — an SEO sliver for approved crawlers, metered APIs for everyone else — does not describe this site. The split here is by hostname: the root is closed to everyone with no API behind it, the content subdomains are open to everyone and need no API, and there is no metered tier to buy into. Nor is there an AI-crawler stance: across nine robots.txt files, not one names a single AI crawler. inferred That silence is neglect rather than permission, and is the most likely thing to change. Basis: the same files still carry Yandex Clean-param: lists of stale tracking parameters, and the only developer portal is a WordPress site on classic ga.js whose newest post is a year old (Probe Log).

The root is not worth the exception

inferred Even for the two crawlers allowed in, the portal root carries nothing indexable. Basis: the 197,086-byte Googlebot-only response contains zero ld+json blocks and its body is a link hub pointing at 14 subdomains (Probe Log). Treat mail.ru and news.mail.ru as separate acquisition targets sharing nothing but a suffix.

Identifier & dedup

News articles are keyed by a numeric ID inside a section path (/politics/72359456/), so the section is part of the key and a re-sectioned article would alias. inferred Those IDs are not densely allocated to this host, so enumeration by increment is not viable. Basis: 1,000 consecutive feed items span IDs 72,285,965–72,359,456, a delta of 73,491 over 14.12 hours — roughly 73 unused IDs per published article (Probe Log). Crawl the sitemap, never the ID range.

Retrieval unit

News: the article, already delivered whole in JSON-LD — no boilerplate stripping, no readability heuristics. Q&A: the thread, not the answer, because the accepted answer and its rivals arrive in one payload with vote counts attached and only make sense together. Film: the title entity, with roles and episodes as satellites joined by the sameAs person URLs.

Cross-modal joins

The video join leaves the domain entirely. inferred News video is VK Video inventory surfaced on a Mail page, not a Mail corpus. Basis: all 1,771 player_loc values resolve to vkvideo.ru, vk.ru or vk.com, and 1,081 thumbnails sit on i.mycdn.me or iv.okcdn.ru, the Odnoklassniki CDNs (Probe Log). Index the metadata here, resolve the media against VK, and expect the two to be governed by different terms.

Freshness & recrawl

lastmod is unsafe as a change signal on the news archive. inferred Part of the corpus is stamped with the date of the event rather than the file. Basis: lastmod values of 1979-02-11, 1979-02-18, 1993, 1995, 1999 and 2003 on a site launched in 1998 (Probe Log). Use the Google News feed for the live edge and content hashing for the archive.

Licence granularity

The terms define three regimes — VK-owned, AFP, third-party partner — with sharply different storage rights, but inferred the machine-readable layer cannot tell them apart. Basis: all 8 sampled articles emitted the same single license URL, pointing at the page that defines all three buckets (Probe Log). Per-item provenance has to be recovered from the rendered page or the corpus stays legally undifferentiated.

Indexing Plan

  • Retrieval unit: the news article, the Q&A thread, and the film entity — three units on three hosts. The portal root is not a unit and should not be fetched.
  • Primary keys & joins: (section, article_id) for news; question_id for Q&A, with author handle joining threads to /profile/ pages; the numeric-slug film ID for kino, with sameAs person URLs joining roles to people. Video joins outward to VK by player_loc, not by any Mail key.
  • Ingestion paths, ranked: 1) Google News sitemap hourly for the live edge — free, timestamped, no auth, 14-hour window. 2) Full sitemap crawl of news (815K), kino (540K) and otvet (≈200M) — free and permitted, with JSON-LD removing the extraction step entirely; otvet is the only one needing real crawl budget and it throttles under burst load. 3) Nothing else exists: no oEmbed, no content API, no bulk dump, and no commercial feed an EU or US buyer could lawfully purchase.
  • Recrawl cadence: hourly for the news feed; daily for the news and kino entity shards, which restamp daily; the otvet archive is effectively static and warrants one pass plus a thin tail for recent ID ranges.
  • Derivatives to generate: Russian-language embeddings and translation on the article and Q&A bodies, which arrive clean. Skip ASR — the video corpus is 1,771 clips of median 52 s whose files belong to VK. Skip OCR; the image layer is social previews and stills, not documents.
  • Demo to build: a per-host crawl-policy differ that fetches the same URL shape across all nine subdomains under six user-agents and reports which host/agent pairs yield content — turning "mail.ru is closed" into the measured statement that only its front door is.

Risks to the Index

  • Sanctions foreclose the paid path, not the free one. The EU asset freeze of 13 July 2026 bars EU entities from making funds available to VK, so no licensing deal or paid endpoint is purchasable even if VK later offers one. An index here is permanently restricted to what anonymous crawling yields, and counsel review should cover the crawl itself, not just payments.
  • The open policy is stale, not deliberate. Nine robots.txt files with zero AI-crawler directives is a 2026 outlier. A single policy refresh could close 200M Q&A pages overnight, and unlike the UA gate on the root it would leave no diagnostic signature. Snapshot every subdomain's robots.txt on each crawl and diff it.
  • Licence buckets are invisible at ingest. AFP material carries an explicit prohibition on storing it on a computer at all, and nothing in the JSON-LD distinguishes it from the freely republishable VK-owned majority. Ingesting the corpus undifferentiated means knowingly storing a slice you are told not to store.
  • The root's UA gate is a naive substring match. The bare token "Googlebot" passed, which makes the wall trivially bypassable and therefore tempting — but robots.txt independently disallows the root for every agent, so the correct behaviour is to skip it.
  • Single-jurisdiction, single-owner dependency. Every host resolves to first-party infrastructure on VK-operated nameservers with no CDN in front, so there is no third-party mirror from which the corpus could be reconstructed if access is withdrawn or blocked at network level.
